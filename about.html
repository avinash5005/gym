About the gym

Built by lifters, for lifters.

Our philosophy

Pumping Iron was founded on one idea: strength is built through discipline, heavy compound lifts and a space with no distractions. We skipped the trendy cardio gadgets and smoothie bars to build a place for serious training.

Whether you are prepping for a powerlifting meet, building an aesthetic physique or testing your limits, everything here is set up to help you break plateaus.

  • Calibrated steel and bumper plates
  • Dedicated strongman area
  • Recovery and mobility zone
  • Posing room with pro lighting
